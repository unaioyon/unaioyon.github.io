Music curation

On top of my work on Econometrics and as a result of a past life where I used to be a professionally-trained classical and jazz piano player, I enjoy exploring new musical directions and digging records (explore my vinyl collection here)! Find some personal favorites below :)

Jazz

voluptueux & lourd

Dense but mostly melodic tunes from and inspired by the 60s, with a strong presence of Blue Note and Impulse! Records.

Listen on Spotify
Flamenco

la luna y el clavel

A (quasi-)orthodox study of cante jondo, the purest and deepest form of singing — beautiful memories from my childhood in Spain.

Listen on Spotify
Crooners

sportsmen

Male voices from all times and places — an ode to love, heartbreak and the paths that unite them.

Listen on Spotify
Pop & Soul

city pop jewels

An unstructured collection of Japanese favorites, for nostalgics of the yuppie lifestyle by the Pacific Ocean.

Listen on Spotify
R&B & Indie

blue & wet

A very personal exploration of the new wave of Korean R&B.

Listen on Spotify
MPB

mpb for parisians

Brazilian MPB classics and deep cuts, as heard from a Parisian apartment with thin walls and dim lights.

Listen on Spotify
Blues

west village blues

Smoky, late-night tunes and spring sounds for wandering souls in the streets of the West Village.
