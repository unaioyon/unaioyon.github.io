Ph.D. Candidate in Econometrics

Unai Oyón

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Econometrics at the Paris School of Economics, under the supervision of Luc Behaghel (PSE) and Clément de Chaisemartin (Sciences Po).

I work on optimal policy learning, panel data models and partial identification.

I also do applied labor research using French administrative data on the universe of unemployment spells.

My interests are many and broad: statistical decision theory, causal inference, machine learning... On my free time, I am an avid record digger and a walker without purpose. And I give recommendations on both ends!
